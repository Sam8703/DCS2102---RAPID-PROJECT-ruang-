========================================================================
       RUANG HOMESTAY - COMPLETE SETUP & RUN GUIDE (STEP BY STEP)
========================================================================

Follow these exact steps to set up the project folder, initialize MySQL,
and run the application on your local XAMPP environment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: ORGANIZE PROJECT FOLDERS & FILES IN XAMPP HTDOCS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open File Explorer on Windows.
2. Navigate to your XAMPP web root directory:
   C:\xampp\htdocs\
3. Create a folder named:
   ruang
4. Inside C:\xampp\htdocs\ruang\, create an "images" subfolder.
5. Place all your project files in the exact structure below:

   C:\xampp\htdocs\ruang\
   ├── index.html           (Frontend structure & template views)
   ├── style.css            (Site styling & printable report rules)
   ├── app.js               (Single-page router, analytics, and gallery logic)
   ├── db.php               (MySQLi database connection)
   ├── api.php              (Backend API endpoints & JSON response handler)
   ├── ruang_db.sql         (Fresh database creation script above)
   └── images/
       ├── favicon.png      (Browser tab icon)
       ├── PalmHouse.jpg    (Palm House main)
       ├── PalmHouseK.jpg   (Palm House kitchen/interior)
       ├── PalmHouseL.jpg   (Palm House living area)
       ├── PalmHouseP.jpg   (Palm House pool/patio)
       ├── PalmHouseR.jpg   (Palm House rooms)
       ├── CityLoft.jpg     (City Loft main)
       ├── CityLoftK.jpg    (City Loft kitchen)
       ├── CityLoftL.jpg    (City Loft living area)
       ├── CityLoftR.jpg    (City Loft room/skyline)
       ├── SereneHome.jpg   (Serene Home main)
       ├── SereneHomeB.jpg  (Serene Home balcony/courtyard)
       ├── SereneHomeK.jpg  (Serene Home kitchen)
       ├── SereneHomeL.jpg  (Serene Home living)
       ├── SereneHomeR.jpg  (Serene Home room)
       ├── HillsideCabin.jpg  (Hillside Cabin main)
       ├── HillsideCabinK.jpg (Hillside Cabin kitchen)
       ├── HillsideCabinR.jpg (Hillside Cabin room)
       └── HillsideCabinV.jpg (Hillside Cabin view)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: START APACHE & MYSQL IN XAMPP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Launch the XAMPP Control Panel from your Start Menu or Desktop.
2. Locate "Apache" and click "Start" (the label background turns green).
3. Locate "MySQL" and click "Start" (the label background turns green).


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: INITIALIZE THE DATABASE IN PHPMYADMIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
2. Go to:
   http://localhost/phpmyadmin
3. Click the "SQL" tab in the top navigation bar.
4. Copy the entire SQL query from above (or open ruang_db.sql in 
   Notepad, select all, and copy).
5. Paste the text into the phpMyAdmin SQL query box.
6. Click the "Go" button at the bottom-right.
7. Confirm that "ruang_db" appears in the left database tree with 
   3 tables:
   - users       (Admin, Owner, Customer records)
   - homestays   (4 properties with image paths & Air conditioning)
   - bookings    (Active reservations with customer contact details)


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: RUN AND TEST THE APPLICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open a new browser tab and navigate to:
http://localhost/ruang/index.html
(or http://localhost/ruang/)

Press [Ctrl + F5] once to perform a hard refresh and load new assets.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: VERIFY KEY FEATURES & ROLE WORKFLOWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Homepage Auto-Rotating Banner:
   - Stay on the Home tab for 4 seconds without clicking anything.
   - The hero background image automatically cycles through the 4 
     properties (Palm House, City Loft, Serene Home, Hillside Cabin).

2. Interactive Multi-Image Gallery & Live Price Calculation:
   - Click "Explore" in the top navigation bar.
   - Click "View stay" on "Palm House".
   - Notice the clickable image thumbnails below the main photo.
   - Click each thumbnail: the main photo smoothly transitions to display 
     the exterior, pool, kitchen, and interior rooms.
   - Change check-in and check-out dates: the "Estimated Total" live-calculates 
     (nights x price per night).

3. Customer Booking (Direct to MySQL):
   - Fill in your Name, Email, and Phone Number in the reservation panel.
   - Click "Confirm & Book".
   - The booking is inserted into MySQL, and you are redirected to the 
     "My Bookings" page with your reference code.

4. Room Owner / Manager Portal (Editing Property & Viewing Guests):
   - Click "Switch Role" in the top bar.
   - Click "Switch" next to Daniel Tan (Owner).
   - Click "Manager Portal" in the navigation bar.
   - Click "My Homestays (Edit)":
     * Click "Edit Details" on Palm House.
     * Change the price or description and click "Save Changes to Database".
     * The record updates directly in MySQL.
   - Click "Customer Bookings":
     * View guest names, contact phone numbers, dates, and update statuses 
       (e.g., mark as "Confirmed").

5. Admin Location Analytics & One-Click Reports:
   - Click "Switch Role" -> Switch to Amira Hassan (Admin).
   - Click "Admin Portal" in the top bar.
   - View real-time aggregated metrics: Total Platform Bookings, Revenue, 
     and the Top Destination.
   - Destination breakdown table shows homestays available, bookings, 
     guest count, revenue, and market share percentages per city.
   - Click "Download CSV Report": downloads a ready-to-use Excel file.
   - Click "Print / PDF": opens a clean, print-formatted page with 
     headers and sidebars automatically hidden.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING CHECKLIST
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Images show broken icon?
  Verify that your folder name is exactly "images" (lowercase) inside 
  C:\xampp\htdocs\ruang\ and that filenames match capitalization exactly 
  (e.g., PalmHouse.jpg).
- Database connection error?
  Ensure MySQL is running in XAMPP (green label) and that db.php has:
  $username = "root"; and $password = "";
- Changes not showing up?
  Press [Ctrl + F5] in your browser to clear cached JavaScript and CSS.
